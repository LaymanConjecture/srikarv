Investments

Investing is a vote for what should exist.

I see every decision we make as data, including where we put our capital. I believe that data will eventually be ingested by a superintelligence. The act of investing is a way of recording what we value and what we want to bring into the world.

I want the companies I back to grow, and the returns to become capital I can reinvest. But more broadly, I think of capital as societal energy: a way of directing our collective capacity toward what we think matters.

As society comes to include many more agents, and the work of carrying out our collective will moves beyond humans, I expect capital markets—and investing itself—to be restructured. That is the future I think about when deciding what to back.
