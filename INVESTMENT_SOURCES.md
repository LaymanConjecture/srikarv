# Investment directory sources

Verified September 11, 2026. The user supplied the investment relationships and the perspective; company descriptions are short paraphrases of official materials. This is a selected list, not a complete portfolio.

- [Goodfire](https://www.goodfire.com/): Builds interpretability tools to understand, debug, and guide the behavior of AI models.
- [Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/): Studies AI deception and develops evaluations and monitoring to make advanced AI safer.
- [Prima Mente](https://www.primamente.com/): Builds biological AI models, starting with understanding the brain and tackling neurological disease.
- [Great Sky](https://www.greatsky.ai/): Develops AI computing systems that use superconductors and light.
- [Conduit](https://condu.it/): Develops non-invasive brain interfaces that translate thoughts into text.
- [Panthalassa](https://panthalassa.com/): Develops ocean energy systems to power offshore computing and other applications.
- [Giga Energy](https://www.gigaenergy.com/): Builds modular data center infrastructure to help AI operators deploy compute capacity.
- [Paradromics](https://paradromics.com/): Develops brain-computer interfaces to restore communication for people who have lost the ability to speak.
- [Radical Aero](https://www.radicalaero.com/): Builds autonomous solar-powered aircraft for persistent connectivity and observation from the stratosphere.
- [Standard Nuclear](https://www.standardnuclear.com/): Produces advanced nuclear fuel for the next generation of reactors.
- [Radical Numerics](https://www.radicalnumerics.ai/): Builds biological AI for understanding and designing biology, with applications in medicine and biosecurity.
- [EigenLayer](https://www.eigencloud.xyz/): Uses staked ETH and EIGEN to secure verifiable applications, agents, and services.

Radical Numerics also describes its biological AI and biosecurity work on its [official company profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/radical-numerics). EigenLayer’s original domain redirects to EigenCloud; the directory retains the investment name EigenLayer and links to that current official home.
